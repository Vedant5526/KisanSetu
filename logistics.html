Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (CVRPTW)

Mathematical Formulation & Agricultural Corridor Optimization Architecture

Mathematical Objective Function:
min ∑ (k ∈ K) ∑ (i ∈ V) ∑ (j ∈ V) c_ij · x_ijk

Minimizes total travel distance and cold-chain energy costs across fleet vehicles $K$ connecting farm pickup nodes to urban drop clusters while satisfying three core constraints:

1. Payload Constraint: ∑ q_i ≤ Q_k (Tata Ace EV capacity: 1,200 kg)
2. Perishability Time Window: e_i ≤ s_ik ≤ l_i (Delivered before freshness degradation)
3. Precedence Constraint: Farm collection nodes strictly precede urban drop points
Clarke-Wright Savings Heuristic:
S_ij = c_i0 + c_0j - c_ij

Instead of having each farmer drive individually to Pune and back ($2 \cdot c_{i0}$), the heuristic merges stops $(i, j)$ that maximize distance savings $S_{ij}$, consolidating 6 trips into 1 unified milk-run corridor.
